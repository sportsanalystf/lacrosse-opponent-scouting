JMU Common Offensive Formations

Early Offense - Standard Spacing

JMU structures early offense with wide spacing (A1-A3 spread along top). A4-A5 in midfield support ball movement. A6 near X focuses on transition. This allows multiple passing lanes and shot attempts from various spots.

Zone Offense with Roamer

The roamer (shown in orange) creates mismatches by attacking open areas in the zone. Typically starts high, then cuts into gaps. Static zone players (purple) move laterally but roamer has freedom to create 1v1 situations. UVA must have a designated roamer defender with good communication.

Pizza Pie Rip Formation

"Pizza Pie" spreads 5 attackers in a fan/pie formation around the box. One attacker positions for a rip/direct shot (RIP in yellow). Quick ball movement around the pie creates scoring chances from inside the 8m. Rip shooter waits for setup, then attacks with a hard shot.

Texas Action / Screen Play

Texas Action is a pick/screen play where one attacker sets a screen for a ballcarrier. The ballcarrier curls off the screen while the screener may slip to the crease or roll to open space. This creates 2v2 advantages and forces defensive rotations. UVA defenders must communicate screens early and fight through/switch on Texas actions.
